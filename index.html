💡 Key Operational Insights & Action Plan
Executive Summary
1 Busiest Windows & Peak Weekend Rush

Friday through Sunday between 6:30 PM – 9:30 PM drive the highest footfall of the week, generating over 44% of total monthly sales (₹91,000+). In contrast, weekday afternoons (2:30 PM – 5:00 PM) experience quiet lulls with single-item beverage or quick-bite orders.

2 Top Menu Anchors vs. Upsell Opportunities

Fries in a Jar (Peri Peri Cheesy Fries ₹199 & Butter Chicken Cheesy Fries ₹229) and Double Xero Shakes (₹269 / ₹309) are the viral high-volume anchors. However, Waffles & Desserts have low weekday lunch attach rates (<12%) compared to weekend evenings (>35%).

3 UPI & QR Payment Dominance

Contactless digital payments represent the overwhelming majority of guest transactions: UPI / QR (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) accounts for 58% and Credit Cards 22% (total 80% cashless). Cash represents just 14%, confirming the necessity of instant table-side QR billing.

🎯

Practical Recommendation for the Restaurant Owner:

Implement Weekend Rush Line Expediting & Launch an Afternoon "Student Hangout Combo":
1. Peak Labor Scheduling: Schedule two additional line cooks and one dedicated fry-station expediter on Friday and Saturday from 6:30 PM to 9:30 PM to eliminate bottlenecks on signature loaded fries and pizzas, cutting ticket wait times below 12 minutes.
2. Off-Peak Revenue Booster: Introduce a special ₹349 "Hangout Combo" (Choice of Any Fries in a Jar + Slider/Wrap + Double Xero Drink) valid Monday through Thursday between 3:00 PM and 6:00 PM. This monetizes empty tables by attracting local college students and remote workers, lifting afternoon ticket sizes from ₹250 to ₹350+.

Data Cleaning & Validation Rules Applied:

  • Zero Missing Values: Every record strictly contains valid order_id, order_date, order_time, item_name, category, quantity, item_price, and payment_method.
  • Rupee Pricing Validation: All item prices are verified authentic menu prices in Indian Rupees (₹139.00 to ₹309.00). Quantities are positive integers (1 to 5).
  • Line Total Consistency: Every row verifies that line_total == round(quantity * item_price, 2) with zero tolerance for rounding discrepancies.
  • Order Integrity: Multi-item tickets share the exact same order_id, timestamp, and payment method (UPI, Credit Card, Cash, Debit Card).

Key Operational Assumptions:

  • Operating Hours: 11:00 AM to 10:59 PM daily (12 operating hourly slots).
  • Menu Categories: Matches the official menu categories: Fries in a Jar, Pizzas, Sliders & Burgers, Wraps & Sandwiches, Pasta & Momos, Garlic Bread & Chicken, and Waffles & Shakes.
  • Tax and Charges: Reported sales represent net menu food & beverage sales before GST (5%) and optional restaurant service charges.
  • Cancellations/Refunds: Zero canceled or voided orders in this baseline operational period.
